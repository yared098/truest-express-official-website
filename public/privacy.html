በአጭሩ / In short:

We collect your name, phone number and — if you provide it — your delivery address, so we can pick up, transport and deliver your item and keep you updated. We do not sell your personal data. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1 Who we are

Truest Express (ትረስት ኤክስፕረስ) is an express delivery service based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, delivering documents, parcels and objects to destinations across the country.

For the purposes of this policy, Truest Express is the data controller — meaning we decide what information is collected and how it is used.

Registered office: Bole, near Getu Commercial Center, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

2 What information we collect

We only collect what we need to move a parcel from one hand to another. Below is the full picture.

2.1 Information you give us

Data Purpose Required?
Full name (sender) Identify who is handing over the shipment Required
Phone number (sender) Call you about pickup, delivery and any issue Required
Pickup address Know where to collect the item Optional
Receiver name Hand the item to the correct person Required
Receiver phone number Contact the receiver on arrival Required
Delivery address Know where to deliver Optional
Item description Handle the parcel correctly and price it fairly Optional
Email address Send receipts or invoices Optional

2.2 Information created during delivery

  • Tracking number and the status history of your shipment (picked up, in transit, out for delivery, delivered).
  • Delivery confirmation — the name or signature of the person who received the item, and the date and time of hand-over.
  • Delivery notes — short remarks recorded by our courier (for example "receiver not at address, called twice").

2.3 Information collected automatically

When you visit our website or use the Truest Express mobile app, we may collect:

  • IP address and approximate location (city level);
  • browser type, device type and operating system;
  • pages visited, time spent and referring link;
  • error logs that help us fix problems.

About Telegram: this website does not use a Telegram bot. The Truest Express mobile app offers an optional Connect Telegram button — tapping it opens Telegram through a link, and you continue on Telegram's own platform. We do not collect your Telegram password, contacts or chat history.

2.4 Information about other people

Important: when you book a delivery, you give us the name and phone number of the receiver. Please make sure that person knows you are sharing their details with us and is happy for us to contact them about the delivery. We use their information only to complete that delivery — we do not add them to marketing lists.

3 Why we collect it

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To perform the delivery — pick up, sort, transport and hand over your item to the right receiver.
  • To contact you — confirm pickup, notify arrival, resolve address issues, and answer your support questions.
  • To track shipments — show the current status of your item when you or the receiver ask for it.
  • To price and invoice — calculate the correct fee based on destination, weight and service type.
  • To keep records — proof of delivery, proof of payment, and resolution of disputes or claims.
  • To improve our service — understand which routes are busy and where we can be faster.
  • To meet legal obligations — respond to lawful requests from Ethiopian authorities, and keep accounting records as required by law.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not send marketing messages unless you have asked to receive them.

4 Our legal basis for processing

We rely on the following grounds:

  • Performance of a contract — we cannot deliver your parcel without your name, phone number and address.
  • Consent — for optional information (such as email for newsletters, or marketing messages). You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest — for service improvement, fraud prevention and basic security logging.
  • Legal obligation — for tax, accounting and lawful authority requests.

5 Who we share your information with

Your information is shared only where necessary. Specifically:

5.1 Our own staff and couriers

Our sorting staff, drivers and regional delivery agents see the name, phone number and address needed to complete a specific delivery.

5.2 Regional delivery partners

For destinations outside Addis Ababa, we may pass the receiver's name, phone number and delivery city to a partner agent who completes the final leg. They are required to use the information only for that delivery.

5.3 Service providers

  • Hosting & database — our website and tracking data are hosted on Google Firebase (Google LLC). Data may be stored on servers outside Ethiopia.
  • Telegram connection (mobile app only) — in the Truest Express mobile app you may choose to connect your Telegram account. This is done through a link that opens the Telegram app; we do not store your Telegram password or receive your full Telegram profile. Once you are inside Telegram, its own privacy policy applies to that platform.
  • Payment providers — if you pay electronically, the transaction is handled by the relevant bank or payment operator. We do not store full card or account numbers.

5.4 Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by Ethiopian law, a court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of our staff or the public.

5.5 Business transfer

If Truest Express is merged, sold or reorganised, customer records may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We never sell your data. No advertiser, data broker or unrelated third party receives your name, phone number or address from us.

6 Cookies and local storage

Our website uses a small amount of browser storage to work properly. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Type What it does Can you disable it?
Essential Keeps the site working — remembers your menu state, form inputs and basic security tokens. No — the site needs these
Analytics Anonymous counts of visits and pages so we know what to improve. Yes — via your browser settings

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may stop parts of the site from working.

7 How long we keep your information

  • Shipment and tracking records — kept for up to 12 months after delivery, so we can answer queries, resolve disputes and produce proof of delivery.
  • Invoices and financial records — kept for the period required by Ethiopian accounting and tax law (currently up to 5 years).
  • Support conversations (phone, email) — kept for up to 6 months after the conversation ends.
  • Website logs — kept for up to 90 days.

When a retention period ends, we delete the information or make it anonymous so it can no longer be linked to you.

8 How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) for our website and mobile app traffic;
  • access limited to staff who need the data to do their job;
  • passwords and access keys kept private and rotated;
  • physical security at our Addis Ababa hub, with sealed and logged parcels;
  • staff awareness of confidentiality and safe handling of customer data.

No system is 100% secure. If a data breach ever affects your personal information, we will notify affected customers and the relevant authority as required by law.

9 Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — ask what information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct — have wrong or incomplete details fixed (for example a mistyped phone number).
  • Delete — ask us to erase your data where we no longer need it for a legal or accounting reason.
  • Restrict or object — ask us to stop using your data for a particular purpose.
  • Withdraw consent — for anything we do with your permission, at any time.
  • Complain — to us first, and if you are not satisfied, to the relevant Ethiopian data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 13. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before we release any information.

Note: some records — such as proof of delivery and invoices — must be kept for legal and accounting reasons, so we may not be able to delete those immediately even after a request.

10 Children's privacy

Our service is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a delivery must be sent for or to a minor, it should be arranged by a parent or legal guardian.

If you believe a child has given us personal information without a guardian's consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

11 Third-party links and services

Our website links to external platforms — Facebook, Instagram and Google Fonts. The mobile app may open Telegram through a link if you choose to connect your account. Once you leave our site or app, the privacy policy of that platform applies, not ours. We encourage you to read the privacy notices of any service you use.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services change or when the law requires it. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will also announce it on our website and in the mobile app.

Continuing to use our service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
